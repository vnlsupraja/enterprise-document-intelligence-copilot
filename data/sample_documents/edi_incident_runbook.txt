EDI Incident Response Runbook

Purpose
This runbook provides operational troubleshooting and escalation guidance for common EDI 850 transaction failures.

E101 - Missing Purchase Order Number
Cause:
The BEG03 element is missing from the EDI 850 transaction.

Resolution:
Populate BEG03 with a valid purchase order number and retransmit the transaction.

Escalation:
If the transaction fails after two retransmission attempts, escalate the incident to the EDI Support Team.

E102 - Invalid Purchase Order Date
Cause:
BEG05 does not use the required CCYYMMDD format.

Resolution:
Correct BEG05 to the CCYYMMDD format and retransmit the transaction.

Escalation:
If E102 continues after the date is corrected, verify the trading-partner mapping and escalate to the Integration Support Team.

E103 - Missing Partner Identifier
Cause:
The required trading-partner identifier is missing or invalid.

Resolution:
Verify the sender and receiver identifiers in the interchange envelope before retransmission.
